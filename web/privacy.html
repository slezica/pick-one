Privacy Policy

Pick One for Android · Effective 30 July 2026

Pick One does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It is what you see, nothing more.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

Network access

Pick One does not connect to the internet and makes no network requests.

Permissions

The app requests a single permission, Vibrate, used only to give a short buzz when a finger is picked. It is not used to collect any information.

Children

Pick One is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
